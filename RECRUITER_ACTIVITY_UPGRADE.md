# Recruiter & Activity Upgrade

## ATS pipeline
- Drag-and-drop candidate stage movement.
- Stage selector remains available for accessibility and keyboard users.
- Candidate cards show rating, tags, experience and application date.
- Every stage movement writes application status history and activity events.
- Candidate receives an in-app application update when the recruiter moves the stage.

## Activity tracking
CareerHub now records first-class activity events for important candidate and recruiter actions, including:
- Candidate: job views, saved/unsaved jobs, applications, withdrawals, profile/resume updates, resume-builder updates and messages.
- Recruiter: job creation/updates/status changes, candidate profile views, candidate evaluation, stage changes, interviews and messages.
- Cross-user events are written to both sides where appropriate (application received, stage change, interview, messages).

The activity trail is database-backed and timestamped. It is not demo data.
